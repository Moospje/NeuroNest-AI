# User Guide

This guide will help you get started with using NeuroNest-AI. It covers the basic features and functionality of the platform.

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

To use NeuroNest-AI, you need to create an account:

1. Navigate to the NeuroNest-AI login page
2. Click on "Register" to create a new account
3. Enter your email address, username, and password
4. Click "Create Account" to complete the registration process

### Logging In

Once you have an account, you can log in:

1. Navigate to the NeuroNest-AI login page
2. Enter your username or email and password
3. Click "Sign in" to access your account

## Dashboard

After logging in, you'll be taken to the dashboard. The dashboard provides an overview of your activity and quick access to key features:

- **Recent Conversations**: A list of your most recent conversations
- **Available Agents**: Information about the AI agents you can interact with
- **Connected Devices**: A list of devices connected to your account

## Conversations

### Starting a New Conversation

To start a new conversation:

1. Click on the "New Conversation" button on the dashboard
2. Enter a title for your conversation (optional)
3. Start typing your message in the input field
4. Press Enter or click the send button to send your message

### Continuing an Existing Conversation

To continue an existing conversation:

1. Click on the conversation in the list of recent conversations
2. Review the conversation history
3. Type your message in the input field
4. Press Enter or click the send button to send your message

### Managing Conversations

You can manage your conversations:

- **Rename**: Click on the conversation title to rename it
- **Delete**: Click on the delete icon to remove a conversation
- **Search**: Use the search bar to find specific conversations

## Interacting with Agents

NeuroNest-AI provides access to multiple specialized AI agents:

### Thinker Agent

The Thinker Agent specializes in analytical thinking and problem-solving:

- Ask complex questions that require analysis
- Break down problems into manageable parts
- Explore different perspectives on a topic
- Generate creative solutions

Example: "Can you help me analyze the pros and cons of remote work?"

### Developer Agent

The Developer Agent specializes in code and technical problem-solving:

- Get help with programming problems
- Debug code issues
- Learn about technical concepts
- Get guidance on software architecture

Example: "How do I implement a binary search algorithm in Python?"

### AutoGen Agent

The AutoGen Agent coordinates multiple AI agents to solve complex problems:

- Solve problems that require multiple perspectives
- Manage multi-step workflows
- Get comprehensive solutions

Example: "I need to build a web application. Can you help me plan the architecture and implementation?"

### CrewAI Agent

The CrewAI Agent creates and manages a crew of specialized agents:

- Assign roles to different agents
- Coordinate complex tasks
- Manage dependencies between tasks

Example: "I'm starting a new business. Can you help me create a business plan, marketing strategy, and financial projections?"

## Multi-Device Support

NeuroNest-AI supports accessing your account from multiple devices:

### Adding a New Device

When you log in from a new device:

1. Enter your username and password
2. The system will detect that you're using a new device
3. You'll be asked to name your device (e.g., "My iPhone", "Work Laptop")
4. Your conversations will be synchronized across all your devices

### Managing Devices

You can manage your connected devices:

1. Go to Settings > Devices
2. View a list of all devices connected to your account
3. Rename devices by clicking on their names
4. Remove devices by clicking the "Remove" button

## Settings

The Settings page allows you to customize your NeuroNest-AI experience:

### Profile Settings

- Update your username
- Change your email address
- Update your password
- Delete your account

### Notification Settings

- Enable or disable email notifications
- Configure notification preferences

### Theme Settings

- Choose between light and dark mode
- Customize the appearance of the interface

## Keyboard Shortcuts

NeuroNest-AI supports keyboard shortcuts for power users:

- **Ctrl+N**: Start a new conversation
- **Ctrl+F**: Search conversations
- **Ctrl+Enter**: Send message
- **Esc**: Cancel current action

## Mobile App

NeuroNest-AI is available as a mobile app for iOS and Android:

1. Download the app from the App Store or Google Play
2. Log in with your existing account
3. Access your conversations on the go

## Offline Mode

NeuroNest-AI supports offline mode, allowing you to access your conversations even without an internet connection:

1. Your recent conversations are cached locally
2. You can view and search your conversation history offline
3. New messages will be synchronized when you reconnect

## Privacy and Security

NeuroNest-AI takes privacy and security seriously:

- All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Your conversations are private and only accessible to you
- You can delete your data at any time
- Two-factor authentication is available for added security

If you need help with NeuroNest-AI:

- Check the FAQ section for answers to common questions
